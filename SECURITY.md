# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting**, on the
[Security tab](https://github.com/Grantor-id/grantor-python/security) of
this repository. The report is visible only to the maintainers until a fix
is published.

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem, and please do not
send it by e-mail: `grantor.id` publishes no MX record, so mail to that
domain is not delivered anywhere. The private advisory is the channel that
works.

Include what you would want to receive: what you did, what happened, what
you expected, and the affected version. A proof of concept helps and is
never required.

## What is in scope

These client packages. A vulnerability in the **Grantor service itself**
(`*.grantor.id`) is also welcome here and will be routed.

## What we will do

Acknowledge the report, agree a disclosure timeline with you, fix it, and
credit you unless you prefer otherwise.

## Supported versions

Pre-1.0: only the latest release. This section becomes a table at `1.0.0`.
